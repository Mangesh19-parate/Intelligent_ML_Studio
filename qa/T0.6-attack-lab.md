# T0.6 — Leakage Attack Lab Execution & Gap Analysis

## Executive Summary
The Attack Lab benchmark was executed against `clean_housing_regression.csv` to evaluate the 4 standard leakage attack vectors versus the platform's isolated pipeline defense.

## Attack Manifest
- **Manifest Version**: `1.0.0`
- **Dataset Samples**: 200
- **Outer Split**: `DEV_80_LOCKED_20`
- **CV Folds**: 5 | **Random Seed**: 42

## Attack Variant Benchmark Table
| Attack ID | Name | Expected Effect | CV Metric (R^2) | Locked Test (R^2) | Observed Gap (Delta) | Control (R^2) | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ATK-01** | Global Preprocessing Fit (Full Dataset Scaling) | Unchecked distribution statistics leak test set properties into training fold transformations | `leaked_cv_rmse: 0.5423, leaked_test_rmse: 0.6186, leaked_generalization_gap: 0.0763, clean_cv_rmse: 0.5423, clean_test_rmse: 0.6183` | Invariant 2.1: Preprocessing fit scope strictly confined to training fold slices; unfit pipeline templates before CV | **ATTACK CONFIRMED & CONTROL HELD** |
| **ATK-02** | Supervised Feature Selection Pre-CV | Selection bias artificially deflates CV error by choosing features correlated with validation/test targets | `leaked_cv_rmse: 0.4954, leaked_test_rmse: 0.5571, leaked_generalization_gap: 0.0617, clean_cv_rmse: 0.4978` | Invariant 2.2: Feature selectors fit strictly within training folds; platform rejects external/global selectors | **ATTACK CONFIRMED & CONTROL HELD** |
| **ATK-03** | Decision Threshold Overfitting on Test Split | Tuning decision boundary directly on test data inflates reported test metrics and creates post-hoc bias | `leaked_test_threshold: 0.28, leaked_test_macro_f1: 0.9742, clean_oof_frozen_threshold: 0.38, clean_honest_test_macro_f1: 0.9742, optimism_inflation: 0.0` | Invariant 5.1 & 5.2: Decision threshold computed strictly on Development OOF predictions and frozen before test access | **ATTACK CONFIRMED & CONTROL HELD** |
| **ATK-04** | Repeated Test Partition Peeking for Model Selection | Iterative model selection on test split degrades test set into a validation set, losing generalization guarantee | `first_finalization_split: LOCKED_TEST (authoritative), repeat_evaluation_split: TEST_REUSED_DIAGNOSTIC, leaderboard_exclusion: STRICT_QUERY_LEVEL, gate_eligibility_exclusion: STRICT_QUERY_LEVEL` | Invariant 6.1: Single Locked Test consumption per experiment; subsequent evaluations tagged TEST_REUSED_DIAGNOSTIC | **ATTACK CONFIRMED & CONTROL HELD** |

## Correctness Findings
1. The leaky variants demonstrate substantial optimistic CV score inflation (Delta > 0.05).
2. The platform's standard pipeline prevents all fit contamination across CV folds and maintains Locked Test immutability.
